# Code of Conduct

Clean the Agent is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Discuss technical evidence and user impact without attacking people or
  companies.
- Respect privacy. Redact personal data, credentials, prompts, and private
  repository details from residue reports and fixtures.
- Accept correction, make room for less experienced contributors, and help keep
  reviews focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact methods
listed in the README. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when
necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and good
faith reporters will not face retaliation.
